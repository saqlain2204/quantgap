What is KLD?

At every word position, the original model gives a probability to each possible next token. KLD says how far the quant's probabilities are from those. 0 means identical. We compute it on the reference's top 32 guesses plus one "everything else" bucket, so it is a lower bound of the true KLD. Good for ranking quants, not for comparing with other tools' absolute numbers.
